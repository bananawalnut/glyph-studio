# Animal motion

Read the relevant `glyph_studio_library(kind="animal", id=...)` entry. Keep the carrier/anatomy, local actuation, route and heading separate. A rigid rotation of one silhouette does not provide anatomical swimming.

Use a quiet anterior body and greater posterior motion for the fish body-wave interpretation. Koi additionally alternate driven bouts and coasts. A ray's wave travels along the pectoral disc; it is not a fish tail wave. A jelly has contraction, refill and glide, with arms lagging behind its bell. A sea angel uses paired flexible parapodia and a quieter translucent trunk. Betta membranes have their own delay behind a restrained body wave. Pisces is a paired-fish motif, not a separate species.

Identify whether a source measures the named species or supplies a related motion model. The library links existing studies; reading a citation in the library is not a new web search. Rates, dimensions, routes and membrane lag in these studies are art directed. Do not claim fluid simulation or measured trajectory replay.

The current bridge can describe these six components but cannot modify their source geometry. For a source-level animal request, provide a concrete implementation brief and state that boundary.
